# Surgical & Interventional Devices

Surgical and interventional devices encompass instruments and systems used during operative procedures, minimally invasive interventions, and implantable therapies. These technologies enable precise treatment delivery while minimizing patient trauma.

## Surgical Robots and Robotic Arms

### Overview
Surgical robotic systems provide enhanced visualization, precision, and dexterity for complex surgical procedures through computer-assisted teleoperation.

### System Architecture

#### Master-Slave Configuration
- **Surgeon console**: Ergonomic control interface, 3D visualization
- **Patient-side cart**: Robotic arms with instrument attachments
- **Vision system**: High-definition stereoscopic cameras
- **Software platform**: Motion scaling, tremor filtration

### Major Robotic Platforms

#### da Vinci Surgical System (Intuitive Surgical)
**Generations:**
- da Vinci Standard (2000)
- da Vinci S (2006)
- da Vinci Si (2009)
- da Vinci Xi (2014)
- da Vinci SP (Single Port, 2018)
- da Vinci 5 (2024)

**Features:**
- 3D HD visualization (up to 10x magnification)
- EndoWrist articulating instruments
- Integrated fluorescence imaging (Firefly)
- Dual console capability for training

**Applications:**
| Specialty | Common Procedures |
|-----------|-------------------|
| Urology | Prostatectomy, nephrectomy, cystectomy |
| Gynecology | Hysterectomy, myomectomy, sacrocolpopexy |
| General Surgery | Cholecystectomy, hernia repair, colectomy |
| Cardiothoracic | Mitral valve repair, lobectomy, thymectomy |
| Head & Neck | Transoral robotic surgery (TORS) |

#### Medtronic Hugo RAS System
- Modular, independent arm design
- Open console architecture
- Touch-screen interface
- Cloud-based data analytics

#### CMR Surgical Versius
- Portable, modular robotic arms
- Small footprint design
- Open platform approach
- Cost-effective model

#### Stryker Mako (Orthopedic)
- CT-based 3D planning
- Haptic boundary guidance
- Partial/total knee arthroplasty
- Total hip arthroplasty

#### Zimmer Biomet ROSA
- Spine and knee applications
- Real-time image guidance
- Robotic arm assistance
- Brain surgery applications

### Emerging Robotic Technologies
- Single-port access platforms

## Laparoscopic Instruments

### Overview
Laparoscopic instruments enable minimally invasive surgery through small incisions using specialized elongated tools and camera systems.

### Access Instruments

#### Trocars
- **Bladeless**: Optical, dilating designs
- **Bladed**: Sharp cutting tip
- **Balloon**: Tissue separation
- Sizes: 5mm, 10mm, 12mm, 15mm standard

#### Veress Needle
- Initial pneumoperitoneum creation
- Spring-loaded safety mechanism
- Alternative: Hassan (open) technique

### Visualization

#### Laparoscopes
- 0° (straight) and angled (30°, 45°) options
- 5mm and 10mm diameters
- HD and 4K resolution systems
- 3D visualization platforms

#### Light Sources
- Xenon and LED illumination
- Fiber optic cable transmission
- Integrated camera heads

### Dissection and Grasping

#### Graspers
- Atraumatic (bowel, tissue handling)
- Fenestrated designs
- Locking mechanisms
- Articulating tips

#### Dissectors
- Maryland dissector
- Hook cautery
- Ultrasonic shears (Harmonic)
- Bipolar vessel sealers (LigaSure, Enseal)

### Cutting and Hemostasis

#### Energy Devices
| Technology | Mechanism | Applications |
|------------|-----------|--------------|
| Monopolar | RF current, grounding pad | Cutting, coagulation |
| Bipolar | Current between instrument tips | Vessel sealing, coagulation |
| Ultrasonic | Vibration friction heat | Cutting, coagulation |
| Advanced bipolar | Compression + RF energy | Large vessel sealing |

#### Staplers
- Linear cutters (GIA type)
- Endoscopic circular staplers (EEA)
- Articulating heads
- Powered stapling systems

### Specimen Retrieval
- Endobags for tissue extraction
- Morcellators (contained)
- Wound protector/extractors

### Single-Incision Platforms
- SILS (Single Incision Laparoscopic Surgery)
- Articulating instruments
- Multichannel ports
- Reduced scarring approach

## Catheter Systems

### Overview
Catheters are tubular medical devices inserted into body cavities, vessels, or ducts for diagnostic or therapeutic purposes.

### Vascular Catheters

#### Central Venous Catheters (CVC)
**Types:**
- Non-tunneled (acute care)
- Tunneled (Hickman, Broviac)
- Implanted ports (Port-a-Cath)
- PICC lines (peripherally inserted)

**Applications:**
- Chemotherapy delivery
- Total parenteral nutrition
- Hemodialysis access
- Hemodynamic monitoring

#### Arterial Catheters
- Intra-arterial blood pressure monitoring
- Blood gas sampling
- Angiography and intervention

### Cardiac Catheterization

#### Diagnostic Catheters
- Pigtail (ventriculography, aortography)
- Judkins (coronary angiography)
- Amplatz shapes
- Multipurpose designs

#### Interventional Catheters
- Guide catheters (6F-8F)
- Microcatheters
- Aspiration catheters
- Support and extension catheters

#### Balloon Catheters

- Valvuloplasty
- Sizing balloons
- Drug-coated balloons

#### Electrophysiology Catheters
- Diagnostic mapping catheters
- Ablation catheters (RF, cryoablation)
- 3D electroanatomical mapping

### Urological Catheters

- Indwelling urethral drainage

#### Ureteral Stents

### Neurological Catheters

### Specialized Designs

## 3D-Printed Patient-Specific Implants

### Overview
Additive manufacturing enables creation of customized implants precisely matched to individual patient anatomy from medical imaging data.

### Manufacturing Process

#### Workflow
1. **Medical imaging**: CT, MRI acquisition
2. **Segmentation**: Anatomical structure identification
3. **CAD design**: Implant modeling and customization
4. **Manufacturing**: 3D printing/additive manufacturing
5. **Post-processing**: Surface finishing, sterilization
6. **Surgical planning**: Virtual surgery simulation

#### Printing Technologies
| Method | Materials | Applications |
|--------|-----------|--------------|
| SLM/DMLS | Titanium, CoCr | Load-bearing implants |
| EBM | Titanium alloys | Orthopedic implants |
| SLA | Photopolymers | Surgical guides, models |
| SLS | PEEK, polymers | Cranial implants |
| Binder jetting | Calcium phosphate | Bone scaffolds |

### Clinical Applications

#### Craniomaxillofacial
- Cranial defect reconstruction
- Orbital floor implants
- Mandibular reconstruction
- TMJ replacement
- Custom cutting guides

#### Orthopedic
- Acetabular cups (hip revision)
- Tumor prostheses
- Complex fracture plates
- Spinal fusion cages
- Joint arthroplasty components

#### Thoracic
- Chest wall reconstruction
- Sternal replacement
- Rib prostheses

### Advantages
- Precise anatomical fit
- Reduced operative time
- Optimized biomechanics
- Porous structures for osseointegration
- Complex geometries achievable

### Regulatory Pathway

#### FDA Classification
- Patient-matched devices
- 510(k) cleared processes
- Quality management systems
- Design controls documentation

#### Standards
- ISO 13485 (Quality management)

- ASTM F3001 (Titanium alloy for surgical implants)
