# Therapeutic Medical Devices

A comprehensive reference guide for therapeutic medical devices used in healthcare settings.

## Overview

Therapeutic devices are medical instruments designed to treat, manage, or alleviate medical conditions through direct intervention. Unlike diagnostic devices that identify conditions, therapeutic devices actively deliver treatment to patients.

## Categories

### [Drug Delivery Systems](docs/drug-delivery.md)
Devices that administer medications to patients in controlled, precise doses.
- Insulin pumps
- Infusion pumps
- Nebulizers and inhalers
- Transdermal patch systems
- Implantable drug reservoirs

### [Stimulation Devices](docs/stimulation-devices.md)
Devices that deliver electrical or other forms of stimulation to treat conditions.
- TENS units for pain management
- Neurostimulators
- Pacemakers and defibrillators
- Cochlear implants

### [Rehabilitation Devices](docs/rehabilitation.md)
Devices that assist in restoring function and mobility.
- Powered prosthetics
- Exoskeletons
- Functional electrical stimulation systems

### [Surgical & Interventional Devices](docs/surgical-interventional.md)
Devices used during surgical procedures and interventional treatments.
- Surgical robots and robotic arms
- Laparoscopic instruments
- Catheter systems
- Stents and implantable meshes
- 3D-printed patient-specific implants

## Regulatory Framework

Therapeutic devices are regulated by various agencies worldwide:
- **FDA** (United States) - 510(k) clearance, PMA approval
- **CE Marking** (European Union) - MDR compliance
- **PMDA** (Japan) - Pharmaceutical and Medical Devices Agency
- **Health Canada** - Medical Device Regulations

## Device Classification

Medical devices are classified based on risk level:

| Class | Risk Level | Examples |
|-------|------------|----------|
| Class I | Low | Bandages, tongue depressors |
| Class II | Moderate | Infusion pumps, TENS units |
| Class III | High | Pacemakers, implantable devices |

## Key Considerations

### Safety
- Biocompatibility of materials
- Electrical safety standards (IEC 60601)
- Cybersecurity for connected devices

### Efficacy
- Clinical trial requirements
- Performance validation
- Post-market surveillance

### Usability
- Human factors engineering
- Training requirements
- Patient compliance considerations
